Did he
actually eat it?

At every crew stop, before you hand him the next packet, ask what he finished. Count the empty wrappers and flasks. He will say “all of it”. Count anyway. Tap once for eaten, twice for half, three times to clear. Then hit Submit, and every crew phone has it.
