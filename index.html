Did he
actually eat it?

At every crew stop, before you hand him the next packet, ask what he finished. Count the empty wrappers and flasks. He will say “all of it”. Count anyway. Tap once for eaten, twice for half, three times to clear.

Carbs per hour, by leg

Orange line is 75 g/h. Striped bars are legs he hasn’t finished yet, showing the plan.

The log saves on this phone only, in this browser. It doesn’t sync. Pick one phone to be the fuel phone and keep using that one. If it dies, the WhatsApp updates are your backup. Carb numbers are from the packets where we had them and estimates where we didn’t (potatoes, smoothie, Coke).
